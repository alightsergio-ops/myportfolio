My name's Barutwanayo Sergio,I grew up in a refugee Camp where life was not easy. Every day came with challenges, and sometimes hope felt very far away. But even in those difficult moments, I had dreams of building a better future. Education became my strongest weapon. I chose to study Mathematics, Economics, and Computer Science (MEC) because I believed it would open doors for me. The journey was not simple. There were times I lacked resources, support, and even confidence. However, I refused to give up. Through determination and hard work, I continued learning and improving myself. MEC helped me develop problem-solving skills and understand the world better. It gave me hope that I could change my life and contribute to society. Today, I see my past not as a limitation, but as a source of strength. My story is proof that where you come from does not define where you can go. I am still on my journey, and I believe the best is yet to come.
